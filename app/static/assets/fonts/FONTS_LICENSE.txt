FONT ASSET NOTICE:
-----------------
The application bundles Nirmala UI TrueType font files (NirmalaUI-Regular.ttf, NirmalaUI-Bold.ttf)
derived for complete complex-script Unicode rendering across Latin, Devanagari (Hindi),
Tamil, and other Indic scripts.
These fonts are utilized by the Nobeth Document Intelligence PDF generation engine
in conjunction with fpdf2 and HarfBuzz complex-script shaping.
